## What OKB is: two descriptions that don't quite match

OKX describes OKB as a digital token used inside its own ecosystem, with three stated uses: discounts on trading fees, voting on platform decisions, and access to exclusive services. That is the definition on the buying pages, and it is framed entirely around platform benefits.

Kraken's asset page gives a different description: OKB is the native gas token of X Layer, a Layer 2 blockchain built on the OP Stack, and sits at the centre of that chain's ecosystem connecting users, developers and applications. This is Kraken's wording, not OKX's, and OKX's buying pages don't mention X Layer at all.

Both can be true at once. The practical consequence: if you intend to use OKB to pay for transactions on X Layer, the gas-token role is the one that matters, and you won't learn about it from the purchase page.

## If you're buying from the United States

OKX's US page states that cryptocurrencies like OKB are legal in the country but regulated, and that users must complete KYC/AML verification to comply with the rules. Buying itself is generally not the taxable event; selling or swapping is, and the gain or loss is reportable as capital gains. Income from staking or rewards may also be taxable.

The guidance points to IRS rules and recommends a tax professional for your own situation. It does not list which states or fiat channels are supported, and I haven't found a reliable source that does, so check availability inside the app during sign-up rather than trusting a list from a third party.
